Good to know

Questions I get a lot

Can a counselor do my temple recommend interview?

Yes. Any member of the bishopric can do the ward interview. You'll then need a second interview with a member of the stake presidency. I can help you schedule both.

What if I need to talk to the bishop about something private?

Just ask for "a few minutes with the bishop." You don't need to tell me why. I only keep the calendar.

How much notice do you need?

A few days is ideal, and Sunday slots fill first. If it's urgent, say so in your text and I'll let the bishop know right away.

I need an ecclesiastical endorsement for school. What do I do?

Start the endorsement online through your school's process first, then schedule an interview with the bishop. Bring your recommend if you have one.

I can't make my appointment. Should I just not show up?

Please text me as soon as you know, even last minute. Someone else may be able to take your slot.
